Your day, printed twice.把一天，印成两份报纸。

Cuckoo reads your mail, code and bills, decides what matters, and lays it out as a newspaper — a morning edition at six, an evening edition at six. Different news, a different front page, every time. 晨昏报读你的邮件、代码和账单，挑出真正要紧的事，排成一份报纸。早上六点一期晨报，傍晚六点一期晚报。每一期内容不同，头版也不同。

Sources来源

From the places you already look来自你本来就要看的地方

Gmail and GitHub on every device; Apple Mail and folders you choose on the Mac. Cuckoo reads them directly from your device and links every story back to its original.各设备都可连接 Gmail 和 GitHub；在 Mac 上还能读取 Apple 邮件和你选定的文件夹。所有内容由你的设备直接读取，每篇报道都能回到原文。

Edited, not summarised编辑，而非摘要

A front page that fits the day每天的头版都不一样

A moved meeting becomes a struck-through time and a new one. A sentence someone wrote becomes a quote. A quiet day is a short list. The editor chooses; the paper keeps one design.会议改期，就把旧时间划掉、新时间放大；有人说了一句关键的话，就做成引语；没什么大事，就列几条要点。版式由编辑决定，设计始终如一。

Act on it读完就办

Reply without leaving the story在报道里直接回复

Open a story and ask the assistant to draft a reply, explain a statement or list what you need to do. Drafts open in Mail addressed to the sender — you review and send.打开一篇报道，让助手起草回复、解释账单，或列出你要做的事。草稿会在邮件 App 中打开，收件人就是原发件人，由你确认后再发送。

Money财务

Bills, pasted where you'll see them账单，贴在你看得见的地方

Receipts, statements and repayments appear as slips pasted onto the page, each showing where it came from and when it is due.小票、账单、还款，像真的单据一样贴在报纸上，每张都写明来源和到期日。

Privacy隐私
Your paper is written on your device, from your sources, with your model key. Nothing passes through us.你的报纸在你的设备上、用你的来源、凭你的模型密钥写成。中间不经过我们。

No account, no ads, no tracking. Editions stay on this device or in your own iCloud. To write each edition, the material it covers goes directly from your device to the model provider you connect.无需账号，没有广告，不做跟踪。报纸保存在本机或你自己的 iCloud 中。写每一期时，相关材料从你的设备直接发送给你连接的模型服务商。 Read the privacy summary阅读隐私说明

Questions问答
1

What do I need to start?开始用需要什么？

An API key from a supported model provider. Cuckoo uses your key to write each edition; sources are optional and you can connect them any time.一个支持的模型供应商的 API 密钥。晨昏报用你的密钥来写每一期报纸；来源可以随时再连接，不是必需的。

2

Do I need an account?需要注册账号吗？

No. Cuckoo opens and works without a Cuckoo account or subscription.不需要。不注册晨昏报账号、不订阅，也能直接打开使用。

3

Will it send mail for me?它会替我发邮件吗？

No. The assistant drafts; you open the draft in Mail, review it and send it yourself.不会。助手只起草，你在邮件 App 里检查后自己发送。

4

Can my Mac and iPhone share editions?Mac 和 iPhone 能共享报纸吗？

Shared editions through iCloud Drive are planned for the signed release. Your keys and connections stay on each device.正式签名发行版计划通过 iCloud Drive 共享报纸。密钥和连接仍分别保存在各自设备上。
